Issue #121 owner review is required before public launch. On November 13, 2026, the public Terms page returned HTTP 200 and still showed the operator placeholder `[full legal name, legal form and address]`. The Privacy page also returned HTTP 200.

## Product copy for review

The upload form explains that submitted photos stay private while moderators review them. Approved work can appear publicly with the creator's username. Creators can remove their work. The form asks creators to exclude children's faces, contact information and identifying locations. Membership already requires adult attestation and acceptance of the current Community Guidelines. Submission does not change the account's optional marketing preference.

Proposed acknowledgement for owner review:

> I took these photos of my own coloring and have permission to share everything shown. I give Lamilia Lomi permission to display approved photos in the Coloring Club with my public username until I remove them.

Use the existing Community Guidelines checkbox for the candidate. Obtain approval for the permission scope and record the approved version against each affected submission before changing the publication contract. Confirm whether galleries elsewhere on the site, thumbnails and related display uses fall within that scope.
